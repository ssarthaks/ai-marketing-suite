# Creative Copylines Research Framework

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This framework provides the psychological and structural methodologies needed to translate brand strategy into persuasive copy. It dictates the specific sequence of messaging required to influence reader behavior based on their state of awareness. Use this document to guide the AI Agent in generating high-converting taglines, ads, and landing page copy.

## 2. Copywriting Methodology

Before applying the frameworks below, the Agent must determine the audience's state of awareness:

1. **Unaware**: They don't know they have a problem. (Focus on Identity/Story).
2. **Problem Aware**: They know they have a problem, but don't know there are solutions. (Focus on Agitation).
3. **Solution Aware**: They know solutions exist, but don't know yours. (Focus on Claims/Promises).
4. **Product Aware**: They know your product, but aren't sure it's right for them. (Focus on Proof/Differentiation).
5. **Most Aware**: They know your product and just need the deal. (Focus on the Offer).

## 3. Core Frameworks & Application

### 3.1 PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solution)

**Best for**: Problem Aware audiences, direct response ads, emails, cold traffic.

| Sequence     | Definition                        | Agent Execution Instruction                                                  |
| :----------- | :-------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Problem**  | State the specific pain point.    | Enter the conversation already happening in their mind. Be specific.         |
| **Agitate**  | Turn the knife; make it visceral. | Emphasize the cost (time, money, emotion) of _not_ solving this problem now. |
| **Solution** | Present the product as the cure.  | Introduce the product as the logical, inevitable relief to the agitation.    |

### 3.2 AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)

**Best for**: Solution Aware audiences, landing pages, long-form copy.

| Sequence      | Definition                      | Agent Execution Instruction                                                 |
| :------------ | :------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Attention** | Hook the reader immediately.    | Use a bold claim, a startling statistic, or a direct question.              |
| **Interest**  | Keep them reading with facts.   | Provide fascinating details, data, or a compelling story hook.              |
| **Desire**    | Translate features to benefits. | Make them _want_ it by showing the emotional transformation.                |
| **Action**    | Tell them what to do.           | Give a single, clear, low-friction command (e.g., "Start your free trial"). |

### 3.3 FAB (Features, Advantages, Benefits)

**Best for**: Product Aware audiences, pricing pages, feature matrices.

| Sequence      | Definition                       | Agent Execution Instruction                                                        |
| :------------ | :------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Feature**   | Technical fact or specification. | State what the product actually does objectively.                                  |
| **Advantage** | What the feature enables.        | Explain how it improves upon the old way of doing things.                          |
| **Benefit**   | The emotional/financial payoff.  | State the ultimate value to the user (e.g., peace of mind, saving 5 hours a week). |

### 3.4 PASTOR (Problem, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response)

**Best for**: High-converting sales letters, deep email nurture sequences.

| Sequence            | Definition                  | Agent Execution Instruction                                         |
| :------------------ | :-------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Problem/Amplify** | State and worsen the pain.  | Similar to PAS, but expanded for longer formats.                    |
| **Story**           | A relatable narrative.      | Tell a story of someone (or the founder) who experienced this pain. |
| **Transformation**  | The exact results achieved. | Paint a vivid "After" picture.                                      |
| **Offer/Response**  | The pitch and CTA.          | Detail the deliverables, the guarantee, and the next step.          |

## 4. Synthesis & Actionable Insights

1. **Select the Right Framework**: Always explicitly state which framework is being used based on the assumed State of Awareness.
2. **Focus on the Subconscious**: Generate copy that triggers an emotional response _before_ presenting logical arguments.
3. **Format for Scannability**: Use bolding, short paragraphs, and bullet points. Never output walls of text.

## 5. Guardrails & Common Mistakes

- **Weak Adjectives**: Eliminate words like "very", "really", "innovative", or "disruptive". Replace them with specific data or strong verbs.
- **Passive Voice**: Rewrite passive sentences into active voice (e.g., change "Time is saved by our app" to "Our app saves you 5 hours a week").
- **Feature Dumping**: Never list a feature without immediately tying it to a tangible Benefit (using the FAB framework logic).
