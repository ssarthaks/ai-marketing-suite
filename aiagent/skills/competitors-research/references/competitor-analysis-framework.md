# [Target Region] EdTech Competitor Landscape

## Direct Competitors (Curriculum Board-Aligned Digital Learning)

### Free Online Study Notes

- **URL**: studynotes.example.com (embedded Notes section)
- **Type**: Free notes and study materials within [Target Region]'s most popular calendar app
- **Grades**: [Local Exam] (Grade 10) and [Local Exam] (Grade 11-12)
- **Content**: Text-based notes, solutions, summaries aligned to Curriculum Board syllabus
- **Pricing**: Free (ad-supported within Regional Portal ecosystem)
- **Strengths**: Massive existing user base (Regional Portal is [Target Region]'s most-downloaded app), zero cost, familiar platform
- **Weaknesses**: Text-only (no interactive videos), no testpaper engine, no teacher/parent features, no diagnostic reports
- **Threat Level**: HIGH — free alternative that covers same syllabus

### Kahoot

- **URL**: sajilopadhai.com
- **Type**: Video lesson platform for [Local Language] students
- **Grades**: [Local Exam] and [Local Exam]
- **Content**: Video lessons, some practice questions
- **Pricing**: Freemium model
- **Strengths**: Growing brand recognition, video content
- **Weaknesses**: Passive video format (not dialogic), smaller content library, less sophisticated assessment tools
- **Threat Level**: MEDIUM — similar positioning but weaker product

### Competitor Notes Hub

- **URL**: merospark.com
- **Type**: Notes, solutions, and study material portal
- **Grades**: [Local Exam], [Local Exam], Bachelor level
- **Content**: Written notes, exercise solutions, model questions, summaries
- **Pricing**: Free (ad-supported)
- **Strengths**: Strong SEO presence, comprehensive text content, covers bachelor level too
- **Weaknesses**: Text-only, no interactive features, no teacher/school tools
- **Threat Level**: MEDIUM — competes for organic search traffic

### Mero School

- **URL**: mero.school
- **Type**: Online video courses
- **Grades**: High school and bachelor level
- **Content**: Video courses, structured curriculum
- **Pricing**: Paid subscriptions
- **Strengths**: Partnership with Telecom Carrier (Pahilo SIM free access), growing user base
- **Weaknesses**: Less interactive than EduPlatform's dialogic format, smaller Curriculum Board-mapped library
- **Threat Level**: MEDIUM — has telecom distribution advantage

## Key Strategic Implications

1. **EduPlatform's unique moat**: Dialogic pedagogy + testpaper engine + multi-stakeholder platform — no competitor offers all three
2. **Biggest threat**: Free YouTube channels and Free Online Study Notes erode willingness to pay
3. **Biggest opportunity**: School/institutional B2B sales (no competitor has strong school admin tools)
4. **Distribution gap**: Mero School has Telecom Carrier partnership; EduPlatform needs similar telecom distribution
5. **Content gap**: EduPlatform should expand to bachelor-level or entrance exam prep to cover full student lifecycle
