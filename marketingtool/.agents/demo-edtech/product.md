# Demo EdTech Product - EduSpark

> **Category:** Interactive AI Learning Platform  
> **Target Audience:** High School & University Students, Educators, Academic Institutions  
> **Key Value Proposition:** Personalized micro-learning modules powered by adaptive AI tutoring.

---

## 1. Product Overview

EduSpark delivers personalized learning paths, interactive practice quizzes, and real-time tutoring feedback for STEM and humanities subjects.

## 2. Key Features

- **Adaptive AI Tutor:** Instant explanations, step-by-step problem solving, and contextual hints.
- **Interactive Quiz Engine:** Gamified practice sessions with immediate feedback and flashcards.
- **Teacher Dashboard:** Real-time class mastery tracking and automated homework assignment.
- **Multi-Curriculum Support:** Aligned with international secondary and higher education standards.
