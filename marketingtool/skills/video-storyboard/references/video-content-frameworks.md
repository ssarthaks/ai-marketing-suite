# Video Content Frameworks

Companion reference for the `video-storyboard` skill. Always re-anchor tone and claims to the loaded `.agents/<productname>/product.md`. Verify platform specs with live research — algorithms and ideal lengths change often.

---

## Platform Spec Cheat-Sheet (verify current best practice)

| Platform          | Ideal length     | Aspect      | Hook window            | CTA placement                | Primary algorithm signal       |
| ----------------- | ---------------- | ----------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| TikTok (organic)  | 21–34s or 55–60s | 9:16        | 0–1.5s                 | ~70–80% + end card           | Watch-time %, shares, comments |
| TikTok Ads        | 15–30s           | 9:16        | 0–1s                   | Final 5s                     | CTR, CVR, view-through         |
| Instagram Reels   | 30–90s           | 9:16        | 0–2s                   | ~80%                         | Shares, saves, reach           |
| YouTube Shorts    | 15–60s           | 9:16        | 0–3s                   | End                          | Watch-time %, subscribe        |
| YouTube (long)    | 6–15 min         | 16:9        | 0–30s                  | 30% / 60% / 90% + end screen | CTR, watch time                |
| Facebook Video Ad | 15–30s           | 1:1 or 4:5  | 0–3s (silent autoplay) | Final 5s                     | Views, CPC                     |
| LinkedIn Video    | 30–90s           | 1:1 or 16:9 | 0–5s                   | Middle + end                 | Views, engagement, leads       |
| Explainer         | 60–120s          | 16:9        | 0–10s                  | End                          | Completion rate                |

Design for **silent autoplay** on feed platforms: burn in captions and make the visual legible without sound.

## Hook Library (first 1–3 seconds decide everything)

| Hook type         | Pattern                                                         | Best for          |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Pattern interrupt | "Nobody told me [surprising fact] before [exam/situation]…"     | TikTok/Reels      |
| Question          | "Why do [X]% of students fail [exam] even after studying hard?" | YouTube/explainer |
| Transformation    | "I went from [struggling] to [result] in [time]."               | Ads/testimonial   |
| Controversy       | "Stop doing [common thing]. Here's what actually works."        | Organic growth    |
| Social proof      | "[X,000] students passed [exam] with this one tool."            | Conversion        |
| Direct problem    | "[Audience] — if you have [X days] to [exam], watch this."      | Pain-first ads    |

Hook rules: never open on a logo; lead with the viewer's problem/desire, not the brand; put the most arresting visual on frame one.

---

## Story Structures

- **AIDA-Video:** Hook → Interest (expand the problem) → Desire (solution/transformation/demo) → Action (one CTA).
- **Problem-Agitate-Solve:** great for pain-aware paid traffic.
- **Before-After-Bridge:** great for testimonials and transformation stories.
- **Hero's mini-journey (explainers):** ordinary world → problem → guide (product) → plan → success.

The product/feature demo earns its place **after** the problem is established — never before.

## Pacing & Retention

- Short-form: a cut, motion, or new text every 1.5–3s to reset attention.
- Re-hook around the natural drop-off point (≈the 3–5s mark on short-form).
- One idea per video; one CTA. Splitting goals splits results.
- Read the VO aloud at target pace — if it doesn't fit naturally, trim words, not time.

---

## Publishing & Optimization Checklist

- Captions/subtitles burned in (accessibility + silent autoplay).
- Thumbnail/cover frame chosen for clarity at small size.
- Caption/description with a hook line, relevant tags, and a tracked CTA link.
- One A/B variation of the hook or CTA to test.
- Seed the first comment (algorithm signal on TikTok/YouTube).
- Clear music licensing (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, etc.) and proof claims before publishing.
