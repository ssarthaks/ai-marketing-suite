# Marketing Psychology for [Our Product]

## Decision Making Models in [Target Region]'s EdTech Market

### The "Sunk Cost" of Private Tuition

**The Concept:** Parents feel they must continue paying for expensive physical tuition ([Local Currency] 3,000-15,000/month) because they've already invested so much time and money finding the tutor, even if the child's marks aren't improving.
**EduPlatform Application:** Reframe EduPlatform not as an "additional" cost, but as a "replacement" that frees up the budget. Offer a "Tuition Replacement Guarantee" or emphasize the time saved not commuting to tuition centers.

### Authority Bias in [Target Region] Education

**The Concept:** [Local Language] parents and students defer heavily to established authorities (Curriculum Board, top schools, famous teachers, PABSON).
**EduPlatform Application:** Always lead with "Curriculum Board Syllabus Aligned." Use testimonials from top-tier private school principals. Highlight the 7 international awards to build instant credibility.

### Social Proof (The "Tol/Neighbor" Effect)

**The Concept:** Buying decisions in [Target Region] are heavily influenced by what neighbors and relatives are doing. "Sharma ji ko chhora le tyo app use garcha" (Mr. Sharma's son uses that app).
**EduPlatform Application:** Emphasize user numbers ("Join 50,000+ students"). Create shareable report cards that parents can post on Facebook or [Local Messaging App] groups to trigger peer envy/curiosity.

### Loss Aversion (The Fear of Failing [Local Exam])

**The Concept:** The pain of a child failing or getting a low grade in [Local Exam] is far greater than the pleasure of them learning a new skill. [Local Exam] is seen as the "Iron Gate."
**EduPlatform Application:** Focus messaging on preventing failure and securing the future ("Don't let your child fall behind in Science") rather than just enrichment ("Learn Science in a fun way").

## Pricing Psychology for [Our Product]

### Left-Digit Bias & Charm Pricing

Instead of [Local Currency] 10,000/year, use [Local Currency] 9,999. Instead of [Local Currency] 500/subject, use [Local Currency] 499. The left digit change makes a massive psychological difference to price-sensitive [Local Language] consumers.

### Mental Accounting & Framing

**Bad Frame:** "Pay [Local Currency] 5,000 for the [Local Exam] Science Bundle."
**Good Frame:** "For less than the cost of one month's tuition ([Local Currency] 5,000), get an entire year of interactive Science classes."
**Daily Reframing:** "Equip your child for just [Local Currency] 15 a day — less than a cup of chiya."

### Decoy Effect in Subscription Tiers

When presenting options to schools or parents, offer three tiers:

1. **Basic:** Single subject ([Local Currency] 499)
2. **Decoy:** Two subjects ([Local Currency] 899)
3. **Premium:** Full 5-subject bundle ([Local Currency] 999)
   _The decoy makes the premium bundle look like an irresistible deal._

## Friction & The BJ Fogg Model (B=MAP)

Behavior (B) = Motivation (M) × Ability (A) × Prompt (P)

**For Students studying on EduPlatform:**

- **Motivation:** Gamification (badges, leaderboards, streaks).
- **Ability (Friction):** Make the videos short (3-5 mins), use simple [Local Language]-English mix, ensure it works on low bandwidth.
- **Prompt:** SMS/[Local Messaging App] notifications: "Your friend Anish just completed a testpaper. Ready to beat his score?"

**For Parents paying for EduPlatform:**

- **Motivation:** Seeing the child's diagnostic report showing weak areas.
- **Ability (Friction):** Must support [Local Payment Gateway] and [Local Payment Gateway] (low credit card penetration). Must allow single-click renewal.
- **Prompt:** "[Local Exam] is 3 months away. Secure their Science bundle today."

## The Endowed Progress Effect

People are more motivated to complete a goal if they feel they've already made progress toward it.
**EduPlatform Application:** When a student signs up, don't start them at 0%. Give them "bonus points" for signing up and completing their profile. Say "You are already 20% ready for [Local Exam] just by taking the diagnostic test!"

## The Zeigarnik Effect

People remember uncompleted tasks better than completed ones.
**EduPlatform Application:** Use progress bars on video modules ("You've completed 80% of Physics Chapter 3"). Send emails: "You left 3 questions unanswered in your last testpaper. Click here to finish."
