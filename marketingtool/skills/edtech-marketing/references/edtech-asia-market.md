# EdTech Asia Market Context

Reference context for marketing EdTech across Asia. Always re-anchor to the loaded `.agents/<productname>/product.md` for the actual product, curriculum, and geography. Verify dates and figures with live research before using — exam calendars and policies change yearly.

## Multi-Stakeholder Map (who really decides)

EdTech is rarely a single-buyer purchase. Design every campaign for one named stakeholder:

| Stakeholder      | Buys because                                   | Fears                              | Reach them on                  |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Student**      | Pass exams, reduce anxiety, keep up with peers | Failure, boredom, judgment         | TikTok, YouTube, Instagram     |
| **Parent**       | Child's results, value for money               | Wasted money, too much screen time | Facebook, WhatsApp, SMS        |
| **Teacher**      | Better lessons, less prep                      | Extra workload, unreliable tools   | FB groups, LinkedIn, email     |
| **School admin** | Outcomes, compliance, prestige                 | Budget risk, vendor lock-in        | BD outreach, conferences, MOUs |
| **Govt / NGO**   | System-level improvement                       | Accountability, lock-in            | Tenders, policy papers, pilots |

## Academic-Calendar Seasonality (the #1 EdTech demand driver)

Demand spikes around exams and term starts. Map spend to the local calendar — these are **illustrative** patterns; confirm current-year dates per market.

| Market                 | Major exam anchors (verify yearly)                     | Peak marketing windows                      |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Regional Market**              | National Board Exams (Grade 10), Higher Secondary Board Grade 11/12                        | Pre-exam term + result/re-enrollment season |
| **Singapore**          | PSLE, O-Level, A-Level                                 | Mid-year + Sep–Nov exam run-up              |
| **Malaysia**           | UPSR (phased out), SPM, STPM                           | SPM run-up (late year)                      |
| **Indonesia**          | National assessment (AN), UTBK/SNBT (university entry) | UTBK prep window                            |
| **India (comparator)** | CBSE/ICSE boards, JEE, NEET                            | Jan–May board/entrance season               |

Generic seasonal beats (any market):

| Period                  | What's happening     | Play                      | Message angle               |
| ----------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Back to school          | New-term anxiety     | Sign-up drive             | "Start the term strong"     |
| Mid-term exams          | First internal exams | Free trial → convert      | "Ace your mid-terms"        |
| Board/final exam run-up | High stakes (peak)   | Max paid spend            | "Every past paper, one app" |
| Post-results            | Next-year planning   | Upsell next grade         | "Level up for next year"    |
| Holidays                | Low pressure         | Brand + long-form content | "Learn ahead, stress less"  |

## B2C vs B2B2C

|              | B2C (student/parent)     | B2B2C (through schools)                 |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| CAC          | Higher (ads, content)    | Lower (school champions it)             |
| LTV          | Lower (individual sub)   | Higher (multi-year license)             |
| Sales cycle  | Hours–days               | Weeks–months                            |
| Trust signal | Pass rates, testimonials | School/teacher endorsement              |
| Best channel | Social ads, SEO, YouTube | BD team, school visits, edu conferences |

Hybrid is common: win B2C traction to build proof, then use that proof to sell B2B2C.

---

## Trust & Proof (EdTech converts on outcomes, not features)

- Lead with **outcomes**: pass rates, score improvements, completion rates, named student/parent stories.
- Curriculum/board **alignment** is a hard filter for parents and schools — state it explicitly.
- Free past papers / free chapters are the highest-performing top-of-funnel lead magnets in exam-prep markets.
- Teacher and school endorsements out-convert any paid creative for institutional buyers.

## Channel Notes (Asia EdTech)

- **TikTok / Reels:** dominant for student awareness — short "exam hacks", "1 question a day".
- **YouTube:** long-form revision tutorials build durable organic demand + SEO.
- **Facebook / WhatsApp:** parent territory — case studies, progress updates, broadcast lists.
- **Google Search:** high intent on "[exam] past papers / preparation" — capture with content + ads.
- **Teacher FB/WhatsApp groups:** value-first only; hard-selling gets you removed.

## Regulatory & Structural Watch-outs

- Data-privacy law affecting minors (parental consent, data residency).
- Approved-vendor / curriculum-integration requirements for school sales.
- Payment infrastructure: local wallets/gateways often beat cards for conversion.
- Device/data affordability shapes content weight (offline mode, low-data video).
